03 — How to capture it

Five steps in the pump room.

The same order as the guide and the portal. Bring your phone and a tape measure.

  1. 01

    Record the walkthrough

    One video per pump room. Photos and video are the best way for us to see your system as you see it.

    • Start at the pump and follow the water downstream.
    • Finish with a slow pan across the return lines.
    • Say the pool name and pipe size as you go.

  2. 02

    Find the return

    Every loop runs the same way. Whatever leaves the last piece of equipment and heads back to the pool is the return. That’s where the device goes.

  3. 03

    Size it

    Nominal pipe size isn’t the outside diameter. A 4″ pipe measures 4½″ across. Use whichever method is easiest:

    1. 1Read the stamp and photograph itEasiest
    2. 2No stamp? Measure across the outside
    3. 3Can’t reach across? Wrap a tape around it and divide by 3.14

    Pipe marked in millimetres? Record it as stamped. The portal accepts inches or mm.

  4. 04

    Count it, label it

    Each pool gets its own record, and each pipe size gets its own row.

    • One return trunk that splits on its way to the pool? Record the trunk and describe the split in Notes.
    • Photograph the existing pipe marker, such as “MAIN POOL – RETURN”.
    • No marker? Add a temporary label showing the body of water and pipe size before you take the photo.
  5. 05

    Note the extras

    Three things decide how the device fits. All three go in Notes.

    Straight run

    Length of straight return pipe available

    Pipe material

    PVC, CPVC, steel or other, and schedule if stamped

    Isolation valves

06 — FAQ

Common questions.

Anything else, call us on site. We’re happy to walk through it with you.

Do we need to shut anything down?

No. This visit is look and record only: video, photos, measurements and notes. Nothing is cut, opened or changed, and the pools stay in service.

Do I need a portal account?

No. The portal is the easiest way to capture and send everything, but an email summary works too. If you’d like portal access, ask your AquaRev rep or email water@AquaRevWater.us.

We have more than one pump room.

Record one walkthrough per pump room, and note which pools and spas each room serves. In the portal, you link each pool to its pump room.

I’m not sure which line is the return.

Follow the water from the pump, through each piece of equipment, until it heads back to the pool. If the system is complex, send what you have. Our engineering team works from the same video and photos and will recommend the best setup.

Our pipes are marked in millimetres.

Record the size as stamped. The portal lets you set the site’s pipe standard to inches or mm, and volumes can be entered in gallons or m³.

What happens after we send it?

Your AquaRev rep reviews the information with our engineering team and finalizes device requirements, total equipment cost, and updated performance and ROI projections for your property. We’ll be in touch if anything needs a second look.
