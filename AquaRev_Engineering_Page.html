Welcome to AquaRev Water’s Engineering & Contractors page.

Your property has asked AquaRev Water to confirm how its pools and spas are set up. Start with this short guide: it shows what to capture in the pump room and how to send it to us.

What we need from you

One visit to the pump room. Nothing is cut or shut down.

  1. 1

    Walk the pump room

    A short video, from the pumps to the return lines.

  2. 2

    Confirm each pool

    Volume and water type. We’ve estimated them; you confirm.

  3. 3

    Size the returns

    Count the return lines. Note each pipe size, with a photo.

  4. 4

    Send it to us

    Through the Engineer Portal, or by email.
